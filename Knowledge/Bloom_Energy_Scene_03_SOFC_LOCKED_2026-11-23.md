## Visual/technical rules
- Opening exactly matches Scene 02's closing viewpoint and camera vector: camera penetrates an external generator wall.
- Device exterior should reference authentic official photography; internal image must be labeled **conceptual/reconstructed cutaway** until validated against actual technical drawings. Not a claim that manufacturer approved the 3D model.
- Correct SOFC reaction: warmed air oxygen reduced to oxygen ions at cathode, ions pass through solid ceramic electrolyte, react with reformed gas fuel on anode side, producing electrical current via external circuit; with fossil natural gas, CO₂ remains; outputs include water and heat.
- Avoid false 100% carbon-free language. 'Without combustion' refers to cell electrochemical conversion, not zero emissions in all fuel configurations.
- '24/7' means a continuous onsite supply design contingent on available fuel, maintenance, loads, and system availability; not absolute guaranteed uptime.
- 'Less dependence on grid' means onsite generation can help address connection delays but does not prove grid independence or eliminate the need for utility coordination.
- Do not add made-up CAD labels, output voltage, wattage, prices, rankings or performance statistics.
- Final 2.8 GW is Oracle **framework cap**, not already deployed capacity: official April 13, 2026 Bloom/Oracle release says up to 2.8 GW, 1.2 GW contracted as of that date.
