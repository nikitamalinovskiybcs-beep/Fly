## Factual provenance and on-screen disclaimer
- NASA Spinoff: https://spinoff.nasa.gov/node/10637 : the precursor team developed electrochemical tech for oxygen / hydrogen resources for Mars, later reversing the process to generate power terrestrially. **No claim of Bloom equipment operating on Mars**.
- NASA announcement: https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/nrppost-feb11.pdf?emrc=3ebc54 : late 1990s research, Ion America started 2001, renamed Bloom Energy in 2006.
- The narrated phrase «Будущее энергетики начиналось в космосе» is dramatic shorthand for lineage, NOT claim of deployed Martian energy infrastructure.
- All VFX images of Mars missions, electrochemical cell and datacenters are illustrative, not authentic NASA footage unless supplied and verified.
